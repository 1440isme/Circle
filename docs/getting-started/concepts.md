# Getting Started: Concepts & Glossary

Key domain concepts used across the CIRCLE platform.

---

- **Circle:** A persistent community space with its own members, role hierarchy, channels, and privacy settings.
- **Channel:** A sub-space within a circle dedicated to real-time text chat (`TEXT`) or voice/video communication (`VOICE`).
- **Direct Message (DM):** A private, 1-on-1 conversation channel between two authenticated users.
- **WebRTC Stage:** A real-time peer-to-peer audio and video media stream negotiated via Socket.IO signaling.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Centralized platform management portal (`/admin`) for user suspensions, report resolutions, and audit tracking.
- **Modular Monolith:** Backend architecture where domain modules (`auth`, `circles`, `chat`, etc.) are co-located in one service but logically decoupled.
